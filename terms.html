Last updated 18 November 2026

These terms apply to the Garagebook website and the Garagebook Mac app. By accepting an invite and using Garagebook, you agree to them.

Who runs Garagebook

Garagebook is run by one private person, called the owner on these pages: the person who invited you. It is a free hobby project for the owner and up to five invited riders. It is not a business, has no customers and does not make money.

Contact: ask the owner directly, the same way you received your invite link. The owner can also see your account in Garagebook's Admin control.

What Garagebook is

A free tool to keep records of your motorcycles: maintenance, fuel, costs, papers, parts and photos. Use is by invitation only, for up to six people. It is offered as it is, as a favour, with no guarantee that it will stay available.

Estimates and information from elsewhere

Garagebook is not a mechanic. Due dates, distances, fuel amounts and prices marked with ≈ are estimates. Specs, schematics, recalls and parts results come from other websites and may be wrong, outdated or for a different model. Always check your owner's manual, a qualified mechanic and the shop before you rely on them, especially for brakes, tires and anything safety-related. Generated diagrams are drawings, not factory drawings, and are not for ordering parts.

Parts results link to other shops. Garagebook is not part of any purchase and gets nothing from it. Prices shown are copied from the shop's page and may be out of date; the shop's own page counts.
