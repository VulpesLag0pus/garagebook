Cookies and storage

Last updated 18 November 2026

Garagebook uses no cookies. It has no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels and no social media buttons. That is why there is no cookie banner.

To work, the website keeps some data in your browser's own storage (local storage and an offline copy). This is not shared with anyone and is only used for the things you do in Garagebook. Under Dutch and EU rules (Telecommunicatiewet article 11.7a and the ePrivacy Directive), storage that is strictly needed for a service you asked for does not need consent.

What is kept in your browser

NameWhat it holdsHow long
garagebook.web.v1Your garage, your sign-in session, the bike and page you had open, and the sync status. Needed to stay signed in and to work without a connection.Until you sign out or clear site data
garagebook.drafts.v1Half-typed odometer and fuel forms, so they survive a reload.Until you save the form
garagebook.fuelprices.v1, garagebook.petrolindex.v1Public fuel prices, so they don't have to be downloaded every time.Refreshed at most twice a day
garagebook.located.v2Only your country, if you allowed location. Never your position.Used for 6 hours, then checked again
garagebook.parts.v1Your recent part search results, so repeating a search is free.Used for 7 days
garagebook.macoffer.v1That you already saw the "Garagebook for Mac" offer.Until you clear site data
Offline copy (service worker garagebook-v1)The page itself and its icons, so the installed app opens without a connection. No personal data.Until a new version replaces it

Other websites

Garagebook doesn't embed videos, maps or social media. Pictures in parts results and schematics load straight from the shop or Wikimedia; those sites see your IP address and may set their own cookies under their own rules. Links to other sites open in a new tab.

The fonts are stored on this site, so loading them doesn't contact Google.

Removing it

Signing out removes your garage and sign-in from this browser. You can remove everything through your browser's settings for this site ("clear site data").

Garagebook is a free, private, invite-only project. It is not a company and sells nothing.
