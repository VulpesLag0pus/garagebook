Payments and refunds

Last updated 17 November 2026

Garagebook is free. There are no subscriptions, purchases, in-app payments or fees, so there is nothing to refund.

Buying parts

The parts finder links to other shops. If you buy something there, you buy it from that shop under its terms. Returns, refunds and warranty go through that shop; Garagebook is not involved and receives nothing from the sale.

Search costs

Part searches cost the owner a small amount per search at Exa. You never pay for them. That is why each invited rider has a weekly number of searches.

If this changes

If Garagebook ever asks for money, these pages will be updated first, with clear prices, your right to withdraw under EU consumer law and a refund policy, before you pay anything.

Garagebook is a free, private, invite-only project. It is not a company and sells nothing.
